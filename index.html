Our Story

Built on Craft. Backed by Data.

Aura Bio·Form Construction is a Portland-based general contractor and design-build firm. We deliver commercial, residential, and industrial projects on schedule and within budget — with self-performed critical work that gives us control over quality, safety, and timelines.

With 14 years of operation and 48+ projects delivered, we bring a disciplined approach to every build. Our self-performed work spans structural steel erection, concrete and formwork, and masonry — meaning fewer trade dependencies and tighter coordination.

Weekly progress reports, GMP transparency, and punch-list accountability are not extras. They are our operating standard.
